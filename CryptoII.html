Course Description The market and regulatory environment for cryptocurrencies has changed significantly in the two years since we did the first iteration of our course. In response, the course will now explore the technical aspects of evolving cryptocurrencies and, to a lesser extent, NFTs. This includes the historical motivation behind cryptocurrency and the roles played by cryptography, blockchains, and consensus methods to make cryptocurrencies secure. The course will survey cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology stocks available for investing, as well as their impact on portfolio risk and return.
