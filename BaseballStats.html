Course Description The fascinating evolution of statistics in baseball. Three veteran OLLI instructors who share a love of baseball examine how statistics have been woven into the historical fabric of baseball since the days of the Black Sox and Babe Ruth. Statistics have now come to play vital roles in roster building, player training methods, and game-time decisions. What might the future hold as these techniques become even more refined? As baseball strategists advance their craft, new questions of ethics and fair play emerge. This course will feature statistical vignettes about the Twins' past, present, and future.
